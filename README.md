# Skyway

Skyway is a small arcade flight game that runs entirely in the browser. You pilot a little propeller plane through a winding desert canyon at sunset: thread the gap, fly through golden rings, scoop up coins, and keep an eye on the fuel gauge, because the tank only lasts so long.

The whole game lives in a single page. There are no downloads, no accounts, and nothing to install. Open the page, press Take off, and fly.

## How to fly

The canyon scrolls from right to left and the gap between the rock walls rises, falls, and slowly narrows as you travel further. Your job is to stay inside the gap, collect what glitters, and dodge what does not.

- Fly through a golden ring for 50 bonus points.
- Grab a coin for 10 bonus points.
- Pick up a red fuel can to refill 30 units of fuel.
- Rock spires jut out of the canyon walls, and eagles cross your path. Hitting either ends the flight.
- Running out of fuel ends the flight too, so plan your route around the fuel cans.
- The plane gets faster the further you fly, and your score grows with distance plus bonuses.

## Controls

- Up arrow or W: climb.
- Down arrow or S: dive.
- Left / Right arrows or A / D: shift the plane sideways.
- Mouse or touch: hold and drag to steer the plane toward your pointer.
- Space or Enter: start a run, or restart after a crash.
- P or Esc: pause and resume.
- The Sound button in the corner toggles the sound effects.

## Run it

The game is generated by `main.ts`, a dependency-free Node program:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes a single self-contained `index.html` (markup, styles, and script all inline, no outside requests) to the folder named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or to an `out/` folder beside the script when that variable is not set. Open that file in any modern browser to play.

## Notes

- The game keeps your best score in the browser with local storage, on the same device only.
- Sound effects are tiny tones made in code with the Web Audio API. Nothing is fetched from anywhere.
- The page is fully deterministic: running `node main.ts` twice produces the same file.
